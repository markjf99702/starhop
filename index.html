Starhop
Hold your phone up to the sky. If the map is off, drag it onto a star you know.

Tonight

Positions of the Sun, Moon and planets are computed in your browser with Astronomy Engine (MIT). Stars, constellations, the Milky Way and deep-sky objects come from d3-celestial (BSD), built on the Hipparcos catalog and SEDS Messier data; cities from city-timezones (MIT). Difficulty ratings are a guide: steady air, dark adaptation and patience matter as much as aperture.
